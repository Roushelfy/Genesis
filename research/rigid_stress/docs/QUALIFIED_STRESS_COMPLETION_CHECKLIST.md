# Remaining proof required for qualified stress rankings

This checklist preserves the full [active goal](QUALIFIED_STRESS_RANKING_GOAL.md).
It is a working completion audit, not a replacement specification or a closeout.
Linked historical reports retain their publication-time scope; terminal result
files and their exact source/input identities are authoritative.

The [2026-10-19 accuracy/throughput protocol](ACCURACY_AND_THROUGHPUT_PROTOCOL.md)
adds independent native production tuning now; fine-reference work does not
block it. Historical research-workflow costs are not production throughput.

The prescribed-pressure case, physical compliant pads and original hard plates
are distinct physical models. A passing discrete elastic core is not a resolved
contact footprint. A passing final field does not validate failed earlier stress
observations. A completed diagnostic or a newly published packet cannot close
the goal while any required comparison or deliverable above remains missing.
