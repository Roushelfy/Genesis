# Native low-shell optimization coverage

This inventory concerns the complete L1 hollow shell, fixed Q10 plus the
declared seven-triangle local contact integration, unchanged final numerical
budgets and actual combined friction variation. It does not claim physical
mesh convergence or the absence of unknown optimizations. Measurements and
rejected attempts are in the [ledger](OPTIMIZATION_LEDGER_20261010.md) and
the [hashed evidence manifest](../evidence/20261010-contact-repair/manifest.json).

The 22 candidate families have measured retained/rejected routes or explicit
profile/cost/backend reasons. Complete two-seed repeated scaling and final
same-source profiles show a practical plateau for this low mesh, device,
workload and accuracy budget. Remaining compute costs are native rigid work,
complete exterior inverse application and contact integration/scatter; there
is no identified applicable high-return candidate left unevaluated. This
does not prove the absence of unknown optimizations. An individual environment's
GPU latency is not separable from shared SIMT kernels; the audits disclose
work-count tails and profiles disclose batch intervals. Full evidence is in
[the final tables](FINAL_MEASUREMENTS_20261011.md).
