plans share those factors across RHS batch sizes. Nonsymmetric pivoted tests
exercise distinct row and column permutations, C/F layouts and strided,
nonsquare RHS. The full egg level-2 model has 9,630 DOFs and 1,920 affine
quadratic tetrahedra. The GPU peak kernel evaluates all four corners of every
tetrahedron and reduces only scalar maxima.

`|K u-b| <= max(1e-11 N, 1e-6 |b|)`. The absolute floor is explicit. Near-zero
freefall RHS can have a large relative residual and a negligible absolute
stress. Their absolute N and Pa errors are retained. A residual target alone
provides no universal output-error certificate.

The old finite Gaussian seed's moment discrepancy was reproduced. The new
vector-traction fit preserves all six components on admitted synthetic
footprints. The live path uses the separately declared constant-ratio
compliant pad law in [PAD_LAW.md](PAD_LAW.md). Source FP32 rounding and the
FP64 conservation budget remain distinct.

0 through 7 contacts across eight environments and the rejected live sliding
snapshot. For that snapshot, maximum complete nodal load difference versus
the FP64 CPU mapper is 1.13e-17 N, moment error 1.32e-19 N m, complete relative
residual 1.73e-11 and peak relative error 1.19e-13. Tensile forces are rejected
and zero-force input gives exact zero nodal loads.

The engine contact getter now exports the effective per-contact friction
coefficient, using the solver's existing pair rule. Padded/compact fallback

## Physical convergence

Twelve fixed asymmetric finite pad workloads were run on full levels 2, 3 and
4 with two wall layers and surface quadrature orders 6, 10 and 16. Positions,
forces, normals and radii 6 to 9 mm are identical across mesh levels.

| Full level | DOFs | Tetrahedra | L/U nonzeros | Build seconds |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2 | 9,630 | 1,920 | 7,489,588 | 1.81 |
| 3 | 38,430 | 7,680 | 42,967,665 | 6.54 |
| 4 | 153,630 | 30,720 | 244,957,099 | 97.39 |

The maximum final quadrature change is 2.39e-7 relative. The maximum final mesh
change is 22.23%, and 11 of 12 cases exceed the proposed 2% criterion. This
physical suite fails acceptance. A continuation to level 5 is running with
the same load law. Wall-layer refinement and live-replay convergence remain
required. Coarse equation accuracy cannot justify coarse physical accuracy.
